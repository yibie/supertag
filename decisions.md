# Decided against

Proposals considered and rejected. Do not raise them again without a new
reason.

- **Recording who wrote a property** (a rule, a model, a Stream agent key)
  in the Store. Nothing would read it, it would stay on one machine, and it
  adds a collection to keep clean. Org text is the only record of a value.
- **Per-tag Stream display settings.** Stream agents already cover it.
- **Query results as first-class collections.** Tags are the collections.
- **Anything RDF-shaped.**
